Request (2026-10-24)

This is very good. The buildings in downtown should be much bigger and the area they occupy much larger maybe 2x in both cases. In general, the hills we see off in the distance should have some lights on them, sparse lights it is true, but lights.

What A6 changes

  • Downtown twice as big (CT58.A6.LANight_city.js, towers): the landmarks are spread twice as far from the middle of downtown, their footprints are twice as wide and deep, and they are 1.7× as tall (the Wilshire Grand ~500 m with its spire; 2× would have put the tallest above your 3,000 ft). The other towers fill a circle of 2 km radius instead of 1 km, twice as wide, up to ~350 m. The commercial area round it is twice as big, and the arena at LA Live moved out with it. To make room, the 101 and 110 now swing round the bigger downtown (the four-level interchange moved to (14800, 14300), its ramps with it).
  • Towers far away: their windows' average glow now varies in blocks of floors and bays, so the skyline reads as dark towers patterned with lit offices, not flat boxes.
  • Lights on the hills: about 20,000 sparse lights over the Santa Monica Mountains and Palos Verdes (single houses and little clusters, brighter than before, no longer thinned with distance), plus Mulholland Drive along the ridge and nine canyon roads (Topanga, Malibu Canyon, Kanan, Las Flores, Sunset Mesa, Coldwater, Beverly Glen, Laurel, Cahuenga) with a street light now and then and houses along them.
